| R-0008 | M3 textures (follow-up to SPEC-0003) | SPEC-0003 says bits 14–19 of an upload segment's first word hold the buffer width (segment width / 64), with the test vector 0x0001_2A00 → TBW 4. On the disc, resource 600's 256-wide segment has 16 in those bits (as read by our code), not 4. Decoding works when the buffer width is taken as width / 64, so please confirm what those bits actually hold (different units, a different field, or an error in the vector) and correct the spec. | Open |
| R-0009 | M7 ground (follow-up to SPEC-0006/0018) | SPEC-0006 lists the level grid's cell-table order (x- or z-major) as open. Our probe (SPEC-0018 rules, patch id 0 only) lands on the level mesh's vertices within 0.5 units for 18 % of sampled vertices with x-major and 3 % with z-major, and gives pack 909's spawn a ground height of 40.5 with x-major (none with z-major). Please confirm x-major, and say what the "origin z" of an outline record is in the candidate list test (SPEC-0018 Ground probe step 3), which we have not implemented. | Open |
| R-0010 | M7 controls (follow-up to SPEC-0033) | SPEC-0033 Behavior 2 builds the stick direction as u = z′C + x′Rt with x′ = side (positive when the stick is pushed left) and Rt = normalise(C × up), the camera right. Taken literally, pushing left moves the hero toward camera right. Please confirm the sign of side in that sum, or the definition of Rt, against the game's screen. Our build moves the hero toward screen-left for a left push until this is settled. | Open |
| R-0011 | M7 walls (follow-up to SPEC-0018 and SPEC-0040 Behavior 19) | SPEC-0040 Behavior 19 says the hero's per-record wall contact over the cell's frame records "is specified in SPEC-0018", but SPEC-0018 only gives the Petpet frame-record pass (correction r − \|a\| toward the chosen side) and the segment query. Please specify the hero routine itself: its height and span gates, how it treats near-horizontal faces, whether the top edge is raised as in the Petpet pass, the disc value or meaning of the model-set entry mask (+0x42) that gates each record, and whether the response is the shared contact response or a direct position push. Our build uses the Petpet ordinary-wall rule with a near-vertical face, a run-plane span of (0, L) and a top more than 0.25 above the feet. | Open |
| R-0012 | M7 doors (follow-up to SPEC-0006 Doors and SPEC-0028 section 13) | The door step runs "when a character touches a door (inside the volume's proximity test)", and SPEC-0040 Behavior 14A says class-1 kind-7 entries "take the plane test". Please specify the hero's touch test for a door rectangle (kind 7, switch 0): which point or segment of the hero is tested (feet point, body centre, motion segment), the distance to the plane that counts as touching, and any margins on the width and height ranges. Our build counts a touch when the body (feet to 3.5 above) overlaps the door's height range, the body centre lies within its width, and the distance to the plane is at most the body radius plus 0.25. | Open |
