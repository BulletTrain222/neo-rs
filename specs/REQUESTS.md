| R-0014 | Combat (follow-up to SPEC-0018, SPEC-0029 10.2, SPEC-0036 Hit detection, SPEC-0040 Behavior 12A) | Those specs test weapons against bone hurt capsules with "the finite-cylinder test of SPEC-0018", and the swept test's last step uses it for an edge segment, but SPEC-0018 has no section describing a finite-cylinder versus finite-cylinder (or segment versus finite-cylinder) test. | R-0015 | Common enemies (follow-up to SPEC-0038 Behaviors 5, 44 and 48) | Five points the build had to read one way. (1) State 2 (alerted search) is described only as "aims at the last-seen point; returns when the countdown ends": what does its handler do each tick, and how does a wandering enemy (states 5 to 7) that notices the hero reach agro (state 19)? Our build only aims at the point, so noticing never starts a fight; only a hit, the alert flag or the emerge of state 8 does. (2) The back gaits (words 3: 23, 26, 32) and the side gaits (29, 30) steer toward a point behind or beside the enemy while Behavior 48 says the facing follows the steered move direction and these clips move along the facing's reverse or side axis. Which way does the enemy face while playing them? Our build keeps it facing the hero (blend 0.3) and lets the clip's root travel carry it. (3) "Play X to its end" in the sequencer, the hit reactions, the emerge and states 10, 22 and 23: is that the one-shot finished flag or a clip-progress gate (0.99)? Some of these clips loop on disc (the Flower Monster's wind-up 33 and emerge 10 never finish). Our build ends them at progress 0.99 or the finished flag. (4) The notice and lost-sight tests use "the sight line between the enemy's and the active hero's sampled points": which points (heights) are sampled? Our build uses half of each body's height. (5) The hold sub-states face the hero with turn speed row 0x84: is that value a steering blend weight per tick, an angle, or a rate? Our build uses it as a blend weight clamped to [0, 1]. | Open |
