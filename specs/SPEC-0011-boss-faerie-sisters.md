## Summary

Three sisters fight together. Each runs its own state machine: idle, orbit the hero, dodge, three kinds of ranged attack, flinch and death. They share one value, the **alive count** (0–3). As sisters fall, the survivors attack more often, fire faster projectiles and orbit faster. The sisters start shielded: they take no damage until a hero shot first hits one of them, which removes the shield for all three. A group dome attack (all living sisters on a ring around the hero) is present in the behavior but never starts in the shipped game (see Group (dome) attack). All timings use the 60 Hz tick (SPEC-0007). ticks = trunc(seconds × 60). "Random a–b" for times means a random whole number of ticks in [a × 60, b × 60] inclusive.

Each sister runs one of three controller routines, under behavior class ids 13, 15 and 16 (SPEC-0029 sections 3 and 4). The three routines share one state set and these tuning values. The controller's state numbers are given in the Layout section so that SPEC-0029 section 4 and this spec can be read together.

## Fight AI build guide

A compact recipe for the shipped fight (pack 1122, character region 1123). Each step points to the detailed section. "Tick" is the 60 Hz tick. f = current HP / maximum HP of that sister. v = clip speed in frames per tick. The group (dome) attack is not part of the shipped fight (its start flag is always 0) and is left out here.

**1. Spawn (each sister, its own controller; High).**

- HP 400 / 400 (monster rows 125 to 127). State 0 (idle), animation variant 0, fired flag 0, damage-taken sum 0, melee-hit latch 0, attack-seen latch 0, dodge cooldown 0. The shared shield is raised (one flag for all three).
- Placement (region 1123, in record order; High): record 0 is King Skarl (not part of the fight) at (18.55, −25.30, −197.13); record 1 is Vanity (class 16, row 127) at (4.38, −25.30, −175.08); record 2 is Spite (class 15, row 126) at (9.53, −25.30, −183.50); record 3 is Malice (class 13, row 125) at (14.86, −25.30, −180.21). The record number does not fix a character object. Each record is bound to the first free character object by the streaming fill step (SPEC-0006): the record nearest the selected hero goes first, and all four (each within 280 units) are bound in the same pass. The characters think in object order, so the order of the three sister loops, of their random draws and of the shared-shot competition is the order of increasing distance from the hero at that pass (High for the rule; the order itself depends on where the hero stands, Medium). Vanity is the nearest sister from every arrival point of the pack. Malice is nearer than Spite when 5.33 × (x − 12.2) + 3.29 × (z + 181.9) is above 0 for the hero's position (x, z), and Spite is nearer otherwise: walking in from pack 1121 (x about −44, z north of about −91) that gives Vanity, Malice, Spite, then King Skarl; from the pack's west-side points near z −114 to −125 it gives Vanity, Spite, Malice. Default if unresolved (rewrite choice): apply the fill rule to the hero's actual position at the first region pass after the region loads; if a fixed order is needed, use Vanity, Malice, Spite. The entry moves the hero to locator 26 at (7.64, −25.34, −134.62), so the sisters start 40.6 (Vanity), 48.9 (Spite) and 46.2 (Malice) units from him.
- Random draws: all draws use the shared game generator. A whole-number draw in [a, b] is a + (u mod (b − a + 1)) for the generator's 32-bit output u, both ends included (equal bounds return the bound with no draw). A float draw in [a, b] is a + u × (b − a) ÷ 2^32. Every percent roll below is a whole-number draw in [0, 100]: 101 outcomes, passing when the draw is below the chance, so a chance of 60 passes with probability 60/101.
- Orbit: approach mode (not yet orbiting) with target radius R0 = a random float in [15, 30]. This is the sister's first random draw.
- Then the four cooldowns are drawn in the order energy, status, group, special, by the shared alive word as it stands (Main-loop launches, Starting cooldowns): after a fresh boot or a save load with no Act 1 escape played since power-on, the word is −1, giving energy 150 to 300 ticks, status 0, special 0. If the escape (pack 1174) was played since power-on, the word is 3 and Malice's escape routine has rewritten the 3-alive energy range, giving energy 90 to 180, status 105 to 240, special 75 to 180 (Controller routines). Group is drawn but unused.
- The controller then waits 5 ticks, sets her invulnerable word (+0x7F0) to 1 for the rest of the fight, and starts her per-tick loop. The word makes every generic hero hit routine skip her (see Hero actions against this boss); only her own hit tests (step 1 of the loop) can hurt her. At start (before the wait) she also resets the shared shield flag to raised and, on first use, initialises the five thorn-hazard records.
- The arena's activate-world routine sets level state 4 when the world activates. While level state 4 is set, the sisters are held: each tick the chooser forces idle and nothing counts down. The arena entry clears level state 4 after movie 52, the boss-fight refill, the hero's move to locator 26, a 0.5 s fade-in and a 0.5 s wait (Arena flow). Count fight time from the first tick without level state 4.

**2. Per-tick order inside one sister's loop (High).**

0. Not in state 9: her hero lock-on record is marked engaged and in range (Medium; Hero actions against this boss, lock-on). At the start of the loop body her alive word for the hero's candidate list is also set to 1 (the hold clears it again in step 4).
1. Hit and dodge test (Taking damage; Dodges). Skipped in state 9. A dodge that starts here ends this step at once, so that tick has no hit test. Inside the test the order is: melee-dodge roll, missile-dodge scan, melee hit test, then the six shot slots in order 0 to 5.
2. Alive recount, once per pass: the first of the three loops to reach this step in a pass (Vanity in the shipped order) does it, after her own hit test; Spite and Malice only reuse the result. So a kill made by Vanity's test changes the count in the same pass, while a kill of Spite or Malice shows in the count one pass later (Medium). A sister counts as dead from the moment she is in state 9. Whenever the recount finds 0 alive, level event 2 is posted, on every pass for as long as any sister loop runs (all three loops run for the rest of the fight, see state 9 below); the level state it sets stays set, so only the first post matters.
3. Cooldowns: energy, status, special, group and dodge each drop by 1 while above 0, unless level state 4 is set.
4. Chooser (Chooser), only in states 0 and 1. With level state 4 set it forces state 0 instead.
5. Rate: P = 2 − f is written (Speed scale). The clip speed of that tick is v = (2 − f) × 0.5 ÷ S = (2 − f) / 4 for every sister clip (all have S = 2).
6. State handler (table below).
7. Thorn hazard tracker, then the shared timed-group step that advances the Mote-eater drain (Main-loop launches; Mote-eater drain). Both run every pass for every sister loop, dead or alive.

The engine then re-times the clip and runs the animation pass. A clip requested in step 6 starts in that pass.

**5. Chooser** (each tick in states 0 and 1; High): special if f < 0.33 and the special cooldown is ≤ 0; else status if f < 0.66 and the status cooldown is < 1; else energy if the energy cooldown is < 1; else move. A cooldown drawn as C ticks at a state's done tick reaches the chooser's test C ticks later. Redraws use the alive count at that moment: energy 150 to 300 (3 alive; 90 to 180 after the escape's rewrite), 150 to 300 (2 alive), 150 to 300 (1 alive); status 105 to 240; special 75 to 180.

**6. Movement** (Orbit): radial approach to R0 until |d − R0| < 0.5, then tangential orbit in a random direction. A new R is drawn whenever d leaves [15, 30]. Speed: 7.5 units/s with 3 alive (10.0 after the escape's rewrite), 10.0 with 2, 12.5 with 1, so 0.125, 0.1667 and 0.2083 units per tick on the path step. The sister faces the hero every tick in move and attack states. Dodges and flinches move her only by their clips' root travel.

Per tick in state 1 (High): let dir be the unit horizontal vector from her to the hero and d the horizontal distance. She snaps her facing to dir, then:

- Outside the band (d < 15 or d > 30; both limits are inside the band) while orbiting: draw a new R (float draw in [15, 30]) and return to approach mode.
- Approach mode (also while inside the band): the target point is the hero's position minus R × dir, the point on her side of the hero at distance R. When |d − R| < 0.5 (checked before this tick's step; strict), she switches to orbit mode and draws her direction flag (0 or 1 with equal chance). The step of that tick is still the approach step.
- Orbit mode: on every tick that her motion record's blocked word or her body contact word is set, the direction flag flips (before the tangent is built). The tangent is the unit vector of dir × up for flag 0 and of up × dir for flag 1, where up = (0, 1, 0): for dir = (a, 0, b) these are (−b, 0, a) and (b, 0, −a), normalised. The target point is her position plus 0.1 × tangent.
- Step: the horizontal unit vector (height ignored) from her position toward the target point, times speed ÷ 60, is added to her motion record's request for the tick; the motion tick then confines it to her mesh. Two conditions come first. If the target point lies on no triangle of her current mesh, or she has no navigation mesh, nothing moves that tick (the step also overwrites the goal slot with a random spawn centroid of her mesh; harmless when the goal is recomputed every tick as above; High, 2026-11-08 enemy code pass). The step is confirmed against the code (High): she snaps her facing to the hero with weight 1.0 in this step, and her avoiding mode (state word 2) doubles the speed. If her own position and the target stand on different triangles of the mesh, the direction points at the route waypoint stored for that pair of triangles instead of at the target (a two-bit flag per pair selects the route; Medium for the waypoint rule), and if her own position is on no triangle it points at a point found on the mesh (Medium-Low). The orbit is a series of straight tangent steps, so the radius creeps outward by about step² ÷ (2d) per tick (0.0004 at d = 20, speed 7.5); it only matters after a long time.

**7. Hurt side** (Taking damage; Dodges): melee hits on a sister use an upright cylinder of radius 3.0 and height 6.0 standing on her position (0 to 6 above it), at most one hit per hero damage window. Hero shots are tested against an upright cylinder of radius 1.5 and length 6.0 whose base is 3.0 above her position (3 to 9 above it), with the shot's own radius added (so a shot that stays low, below about 2 above her feet, misses). The melee dodge is rolled at most once per hero attack. The missile dodge is rolled per nearby hero shot, with a 60, 120 or 180-tick scan cooldown; "nearby" means the shot's next 30 ticks of flight cross an upright cylinder of radius 2.0 and length 6.0 standing on her position. Damage only after the shield is broken by a shot. Damage taken: melee trunc(weapon A × Mote factors × step factor × petpet factor × status factor) with the attack-power table not used; shots trunc(S × C × T) (Hero actions against this boss).

**8. Phases:** none beyond the strict HP bands 0.66 and 0.33 per sister and the alive count (dodge chances, projectile speed, move speed and cooldown ranges). Nothing resets when a band is crossed.

**9. Arena hand-offs** (Arena flow): level state 4 holds the sisters until the entry finishes. Level event 1 (first shield break) leads to movie 53. Level event 2 (all dead) leads to the boss end and movie 54.

**10. Death:** state 9 plays action 26. The alive count drops at the next recount (same pass for a kill made by the first loop of the pass, otherwise one pass later; step 2). The other sisters then use the smaller alive count, which gives them faster shots, faster movement and different dodge chances. A dead sister is not removed and her loop never stops: at the end of the death clip she stays in state 9 on its last frame for the rest of the fight (Edge cases for implementers).

**11. Edge cases for implementers** (the settled points that are easy to miss; each is detailed in the sections named):

1. Percent rolls are whole-number draws in [0, 100] (101 outcomes), passing when below the chance: the 60 % melee dodge passes with 60/101, the 75 % missile dodge with 75/101. Cooldown draws are inclusive too (150 to 300 ticks is 151 outcomes).
2. The melee-dodge latch is set on the first tick the hero is in an attack state while she is in state 0 or 1, before the facing, distance and roll tests, and stays set until a tick on which the hero is not in an attack state. If she is far away, or facing away, or busy (states 2 to 9) on that tick, there is no roll for that whole attack even if the hero walks into range later. Roberta's shots never start it (only her swats do; Hero actions against this boss).
3. The missile-dodge scan puts the 60 to 180-tick cooldown on as soon as one shot passes the proximity test, before the roll, so a failed roll still blocks further missile dodges for that time. A second shot in the same tick that also passes redraws the cooldown and rolls again. The scan runs only in states 0 and 1 (never while she attacks, flinches or dodges).
4. Order inside a tick: hit test before chooser before handler. A damaging hit on the same tick as the release cancels the launch; the hit puts her in state 8 and the attack's cooldown is not redrawn, so she repeats the attack after the flinch. A dodge start ends the hit test for that tick.
5. After a damaging hit (state 8) every further hit is absorbed until the flinch ends (29 to 108 ticks by HP fraction): melee hits still consume the melee-hit latch, shots are still removed, nothing else happens. The same holds in the dodge states. With the shield up, such hits still flash 4008 / break the shield.
6. A hit of 0 damage (an unarmed hero, or a product that truncates to 0) once the shield is down still flinches her, plays the hit flash and sets the melee-hit latch. (Only reachable with no weapon or a very weak stat.)
7. A hit whose damage is more than her current HP kills her without touching HP, with no flinch and no hit flash. A hit equal to her current HP goes through the flinch path: HP becomes 0, the shared death hook runs (clears her statuses; the rows give no drops, so nothing spawns) and her base state is set back to normal, then she enters state 9. Both end the same way in play: state 9, the death clip and then nothing (Medium-High for the exact-kill path).
8. A dead sister keeps running: the loop never leaves state 9, nothing removes her, and her body stays on the last frame of the death clip. Her hero-candidate alive word and body-push flag are cleared at the end of the clip. The Mote-eater drain is therefore stepped three times per tick for the whole fight, however many sisters are dead.
9. The alive count that decides dodge chance, projectile speed, move speed and cooldown draws is read at the moment of use. At the very first loop pass it can still be −1 or 0 (chance 0, speeds from the base word) because the hit test runs before the first recount.
10. The tangent direction flag flips on every tick the blocked or contact word is set (up to 120 ticks after one boundary push); do not collapse it to one flip.
11. Level event 2 is posted on every pass while the count is 0; it is a level state set, so it is idempotent. Level state 4 (hold) is never set again after the entry clears it.
12. The sisters read only the selected hero's position and attack state. Nothing checks the hero's death, reach or navigation mesh; a target point that lies on no triangle of her mesh gives no step at all that tick (a target on the mesh but beyond a wall is still pushed back by the motion step, which may set the blocked word and flip her orbit direction).
13. The attack clips share one clip, so a chosen attack right after another attack's end fizzles (see the chained-attack quirk vector below). The same applies to a dodge or flinch that starts on the tick right after one of the same variant ended.
14. Roberta's low shots can miss (the shot cylinder starts 3.0 above her position, so a shot flying level near the floor only grazes or passes under it); a shot locked on a sister aims at her target point, which is the shared default (attachment point 7; Medium).

- Alternate-loop ball: 0.5 per tick, life 240 ticks (120 units of reach), aimed at the hero + 2.0 (or at a named target) plus a random aim error of up to 8.0 (plus or minus) vertically and 8.0 sideways, two independent draws. The mode argument comes from the flag at the sister's +0x60, which is 1 only for 40 percent rolls with the global target word set (Medium). So the ball can miss a hero who hangs or climbs.
- Thorn hazard: left where a thorn shot ended, contact radius 1.0 against the hero, 10 damage, then 120 ticks (twice the tick-rate word) before it can hurt again; three melee strikes (weapon radius 1.0, length 2.5) remove it after its 60-tick growth (progress step 1/60 per tick). High (confirmed against the code, 2026-11-08). Extra facts: the thorn also ends when it comes within (slack + 1.0) of its linked origin point; the strike count rises only while the hero is in an attack state and a strike is not already latched; the hit uses attacker -1 and mode 3, and the damage word at +0xBC is set by the spawner. This is a different object from the Gelert Assassin light arc of SPEC-0034 section 19.3.
- Status and drain effects (poison, curse, slow, Mote eater, leach) ride on the same shots and have no reach of their own.
- Distances the AI uses: the orbit keeps each sister at a radius R drawn from 15 to 30 around the hero (she moves radially until within 0.5 of R, then circles; a body contact or navmesh push flips the circling side); melee dodge when the hero starts an attack within 7.0 and in front of her (dot above 0.5); missile dodge when a shot's 30-tick path meets her upright cylinder radius 2.0, 0 to 6 above her feet. The intended dome (unreachable) uses a ring of radius 25 and a dome of 20.
- Hurt shapes (for the hero's reach): the hero's swing is tested against one upright cylinder radius 3.0 from her feet to 6.0 up; a hero shot against a cylinder radius 1.5 from 3.0 to 9.0 above her feet, so a level shot aimed at her knees passes under it.
- Safe spots: none fixed; distance helps only through the travel time (the farther the sister, the more time to step aside), and the 15 to 30 orbit keeps every shot at least 15 units long unless the hero closes in.
- Worked example: three sisters alive, one 20.0 away releases an energy ball on tick r while the hero stands still: the ball covers 0.5 per tick and meets him on about r + 37 (from 2.5 up, aimed 2.0 up, the line ends at his capsule), 25 damage reduced by his shield and armour. If he starts walking sideways on r + 1, he is clear of the line by r + 11 and the ball flies past.

## Player summary

- The three sisters (400 health each) share one shield. While it is up nothing hurts them: Tor's swings only flash on it and throw Tor back. The first hit by one of Roberta's shots (a quick shot or a full release) breaks it for good; that shot takes no health, so play Roberta first and shoot a sister.
- After the shield is broken, a sister takes Tor's sword swings, Roberta's staff swats and Roberta's shots, each when the hit lands in a damage window and she is not dodging. Motes, Mote attacks, beams, bubbles and spells never hurt a sister, and potions do nothing to her. All three must be defeated.
- Avoid her energy balls and missiles: a ball crosses 20 units in about 37 ticks, so step sideways early. A shield held facing a ball cuts it to a quarter; only a shield Mote of the right element sends a ball back, and then it hurts nobody.

## Behavior

### Shared bookkeeping (each tick)

- alive = the number of sisters not in the dead state. It is the run-time word at data 0x36DC64 (outside the file image), which a start-up initialiser sets to -1 at boot (SPEC-0026, SPEC-0029). It is recomputed once per pass: the bookkeeping keeps the last pass counter it saw (the game's pass counter at 0x368F98, +1 at the end of every pass) and recounts only when the counter has changed, so several sisters calling it in one pass recount once, by the first sister loop to reach the call in that pass (Vanity in the shipped order, which runs after her own hit test; High). When the recount finds 0, the fight ends: the bookkeeping posts level event 2 (all sisters dead). It posts it on every pass in which the recount runs and finds 0, which is every pass for the rest of the fight because the sister loops never stop; the level state it sets is already set after the first post, so the repeats change nothing.
- The same bookkeeping stores 0 into the group-attack start flag (the word at data 0x36DC68) every frame before testing it, so the group attack never starts (see Group (dome) attack). High.
- The alive count is also the shared group word: the ring-slot rule (below) tests it against 3. The alternate loop that writes 3 to it at its start (see Controller routines) is overwritten by the next frame's recomputation.
- Shared block layout (2026-11-09, High, from the code; the three sisters share one block and each record is 0x90 bytes): word 0x00 is the pass counter the block was last refreshed for; word 0x04 is the living-sister count (3, 2 or 1) and is the word at data 0x36DC64 (it is not a difficulty; it selects the walk speed, the shot speed and the dome ring slots); word 0x08 is the dome-start request (zeroed by every refresh and tested by the state chooser); word 0x0C is the NEXT DOME-SLOT INDEX (0, 1 or 2) handed to a sister that enters the dome state (it is not a damage meter; the damage a sister has taken lives in her own record, field 0x0C of the sister record); word 0x10 counts ticks since the dome started; the sister records follow at 0x20 (Vanity), 0xB0 (Malice) and 0x140 (Spite); word 0x1D0 points to the dome-centre object and word 0x1D4 is the shield-is-down flag (cleared by each controller at its start, set by the first hero missile hit). The per-record kind index (byte 8) is 0 Malice, 1 Spite, 2 Vanity (High; fixed from the names each main loop prints at its start; the earlier files had kinds 1 and 2 swapped).

**Per-sister projectile kind.** On entry, the three controller routines initialize their shared kind selector at block +0x08 to 0, 1 and 2, respectively: class 13 uses kind 0, class 15 uses kind 1, and class 16 uses kind 2. The same selector selects both the state-5 status missile and state-6 special missile (SPEC-0029, Behavior 4). These assignments map class 13 to Malice (row 125, type 53), class 15 to Spite (row 126, type 54), and class 16 to Vanity (row 127, type 55), matching the placement data (SPEC-0029, Open question 138; High). Therefore their state-5/status and state-6/special pairs are Malice: poison/thorn, Spite: curse/leach, and Vanity: slow/Mote-eater.

**Chooser** (in idle or move only; High). Let f = HP / max HP. The first rule that holds applies:

- If f < 0.33 and the special cooldown is 0 or less: special missile (state 6).
- Otherwise, if f < 0.66 and the status cooldown is below 1: status missile (state 5).
- Otherwise, if the energy cooldown is below 1: energy ball (state 4).
- Otherwise: move (state 1).

So below 0.33 a sister picks the special missile when its cooldown has expired, and otherwise falls through to the middle band. All three sister controllers handle state 6. The cooldowns count down by 1 per tick unless level state 4 is set. The special cooldown is drawn uniformly from 1.25 to 3.0 s (75 to 180 ticks).

**Speed scale.** Before the state handler each tick, the sister's class routine writes P = 2 − f, where f = current HP / maximum HP. The NPC update has already reset P to 1.0 and applied status factors; this direct write replaces that accumulated P value. Clip re-time follows the class routine, so the rate for that tick is R = character speed scale × (2 − f). At character speed scale 1.0, the factor is 1 at full HP, 1.5 at half HP and 2 at zero HP. The root step evaluated at that rate is used by the later motion-record pass.

**Dodges** (the first part of the per-tick hit and dodge test; skipped in state 9; High):

- **Attack-seen latch.** While the hero's attack-state test (SPEC-0040, Behavior 18) is false, the sister clears her attack-seen latch and her melee-hit latch.
- **Melee dodge, once per hero attack.** On the first tick on which the hero is in an attack state while the sister is in state 0 or 1, the latch is set. Then, only if the dot product of the unit horizontal direction from the sister to the hero with the sister's facing is above 0.5, and the horizontal distance is below 7.0, a whole-number draw of 0 to 100 (101 outcomes) is compared with the melee-dodge chance for the alive count (40 / 50 / 60 %, read at that moment; passes when the draw is below it). On a pass: state 3, animation variant a draw of 0 or 1, and the tick's test ends. Because of the latch, a long attack is checked only on its first eligible tick. The melee dodge does not start any cooldown.
- **Missile dodge.** Only while the dodge cooldown is below 1 and the sister is in state 0 or 1. Each of the hero's six shot slots that is active is tested: the segment from the shot's position to its position plus 30 times its per-tick velocity (0.5 s of flight) is tested against an upright finite cylinder of radius 2.0 and full length 6.0 whose centre is 3.0 above the sister's position (so it spans 0 to 6 above her, flat ends; High for the radius and length, Medium-High for the end-cap rule). The test hits when the segment enters the cylinder, or when it starts inside it. On a pass, the dodge cooldown is set to a draw of 1 to 3 times 60 ticks first. Then a draw of 0 to 100 below the missile-dodge chance (50 / 60 / 75 %) gives state 2 with a variant draw, and the tick's test ends. On a failed roll the scan goes on to the next slot, which may redraw the cooldown.
- **Effect of a dodge.** Action 13 or 15 (variant 0) is the mirrored entry of 14 or 16 (variant 1), so the dodge goes to one side or the other. The missile dodge travels 6.12 units sideways. The melee dodge travels 5.93 sideways and 5.12 backward. Both travel by the clip's root motion (Fight AI build guide, step 3). A dodging sister takes no damage (Taking damage).

**Orbit** (move state):

- If the horizontal distance d from the sister to the hero (x and z only) is outside [15, 30], pick a target radius R = random in [15, 30] and move radially until |d − R| < 0.5. (Confidence: High for the band and the distance test.)
- Then circle the hero sideways in a random direction. On every tick on which the sister's body contact flag or her navmesh blocked flag (SPEC-0056) is set, the direction flips. The blocked flag stays set for 120 ticks after one push by a navmesh edge (or one restore onto the mesh), so a sister pushed by an arena edge reverses on each of those ticks, up to 120 flips, and makes no net sideways progress until the flag clears (quirk, reproduce; see Edge cases). (Confidence: Medium-High.)
- Speed (High): the path-following move step uses 12.5 units/s with 1 alive and 10.0 with 2 alive (code constants), and otherwise the word 0x2DAB50 (7.5 on disc); per tick it passes speed / 60 to the shared route step. Malice's alternate routine overwrites 0x2DAB50 with 10.0 (see Controller routines). In orbit mode, 0.1 units per tick changes the tangential waypoint; the actor then follows that waypoint through the same route step, subject to its speed and collision response. [High for waypoint and speed inputs; actual displacement depends on the route step.]

### Main-loop launches, element, status values and starting cooldowns (High unless marked)

The energy ball (state 4), the three status missiles (state 5) and the three special missiles (state 6) of the main loop are launched in the same way:

- **Launch point and aim.** The launch point is the sister's attachment point raised 2.5 units, the same point the alternate loop uses. The aim point is the hero's position raised 2.0 units. The main loop has no target selector and no random aim offset; those belong to the alternate loop only. The velocity is the unit vector from the launch point to the aim point times the speed word for the alive count: 0.75 with 2 alive, 0.9 with 1 alive, and 0.5 otherwise (3 alive, and also the boot value -1 and the value 0).
- **Release.** A projectile is launched on the first tick on which the release event (bit 0x20) is active or the animation's progress value is above 0.5, whichever comes first, provided the sister's fired flag is clear (progress rule: Medium-High). The flag is set at that moment, even if the pool has no free slot; in that case nothing is launched. The flag is cleared when the next attack state is entered.
- **Element.** Every main-loop projectile (energy ball, the three status missiles, thorn, leach and Mote-eater) carries element -1, meaning no element recorded. It is not 0. The alternate-loop ball is the only sister projectile with a positive element (6). Under the pool rules (SPEC-0029 Behavior 9) -1 counts as a nonzero element that equals no Mote element, so only an all-element shield Mote (kind 7) reflects a main-loop energy ball; the missiles of kinds 11 to 16 are never reflected. The breaks-objects flag is 0 for the main-loop projectiles (the alternate-loop ball sets it to 1).
- **Status values.** The poison, curse and slow missiles carry status ids 2, 1 and 3 with chance 100. The setup record's value field is never written by the sisters, so it keeps its default of -1, and the two extra words of the status request are also -1. The hero's status apply (SPEC-0036) reads -1 as the status's default duration: poison 120 s, curse 60 s, slow 60 s. Thorn, leach and Mote-eater carry no status.
- **Starting cooldowns.** When a sister's controller starts, it draws four cooldowns by the alive-count rule and sets the dodge cooldown to 0. The draw order is energy, status, group, special, each as a whole number of ticks (seconds x 60, truncated) drawn uniformly and inclusively; a draw whose two bounds are equal returns that bound without using the random stream. The selector for the energy ball treats alive -1 like 3 alive (bounds 150 and 300 ticks); the selectors for status, special and group give bounds of 0 and 0 for alive -1 and for alive 0. At the first start after boot the shared alive word is -1, so each sister starts with: energy cooldown a uniform whole number from 150 to 300, status cooldown 0, special cooldown 0, group interval 0, dodge cooldown 0 (High). With the alive word at 1, 2 or 3 the draws are energy 150 to 300, status 105 to 240 and special 75 to 180 (the disc values are equal for all three alive counts), and group per the table above. With the alive word at 0 all four are 0. The alive word keeps its last value between fights in one session (0 after a won fight), so a later start uses that value (Medium-High). Because the chooser needs the energy cooldown below 1, the status cooldown below 1 and the special cooldown at 0 or less, a first-start sister in the 0.33 to 0.66 band fires a status missile on her first chooser pass, while one above 0.66 cannot fire an energy ball for 150 to 300 ticks.
- **Thorn hits and the thorn hazard (High).** The thorn shot is an ordinary pool shot: its first hit on the hero deals 10 once and frees the slot (a ground hit or the end of its life frees it too). The "up to 3 hits" word (0x2DAB84 = 3) belongs to a second object, a thorn hazard from the five-record thorn pool (SPEC-0029 Layout 1a). On the first tick on which a thorn shot's slot is free again, the sister whose kind selector is 0 claims the first free hazard record (none free: nothing spawns) and places it at the shot's last horizontal position. **Spawn height (High):** the height is the ground height returned by the world's downward height probe, started 8.0 above the shot's last height at that horizontal position; the probe returns the highest ground surface at or below its start, so the hazard stands on the floor under the shot (or on a ledge up to 8.0 above the shot's last height), whatever the height the shot ended at. If the probe finds no surface it returns 0.0 for a position outside the loaded ground cells (Medium for that fallback). **Lifecycle (High for the order and the numbers, once per tick for the whole pool, not once per sister):** each tick the hazard first clears its hit-this-tick flag, then adds 1/60 to its growth value (the growth value starts at 0). While growth is below 1.0 (60 ticks) the hazard is still growing; when it reaches 1.0 the growing state ends and the model scale is set to 1.0. The contact test runs on every tick of the hazard's life, growing or not: when the contact cooldown is 0 or less and the hero's body is touched (a body test of radius 1.0, word 0x2DCB20, against the hazard's vertical line), the hero takes a hit of 10 (word 0x2DAB80; source -1, hit kind 3) and the cooldown is set to 120 ticks. The cooldown counts down by 1 per tick only while it is above 0, so once it has run out the next touch hurts at once. Strike counting runs only after the growth ends: while the hero's attack-active test is true and the strike latch is clear, a hero weapon volume (radius 1.0, length 2.5) that reaches the hazard counts one strike (latch set, strike counter plus 1, a hit spark at 1.0 above the hazard and a hit sound); the latch clears on the first tick on which the attack-active test is false, so each swing counts once. On any tick on which the strike counter has reached the hit limit (word 0x2DAB84 = 3), the hazard is removed: its model is released, the record becomes free for a new thorn and a removal effect (id 470) plays at its position. There is no time limit, so a hazard stays until it is struck out or the world is torn down; when all five records are in use, further thorn shots leave nothing. The other kinds (leach, Mote-eater) leave nothing behind. Test vector: a thorn shot ends at (10, 4.0, -6) over a floor at 0.0: the probe starts at height 12.0, finds the floor, and the hazard stands at (10, 0.0, -6); it hurts the hero at tick 0 of its life if he stands within 1.0, then at ticks 120, 240 and so on while he stays; three swings after tick 60 remove it.

**Mote-eater drain (kind 14; High, rules owned by SPEC-0034 3.13).** When a Mote-eater missile hits the hero and is not blocked, and no drain is running, the hit starts the drain. SPEC-0034 3.13 gives the full rules and data words; in outline:

- 8 picks from the twelve Mote ids 160 to 171, each a uniform whole number from 0 to 11 that steps to (pick mod 12) + 1 while it repeats an earlier pick. Quirk: a pick that steps past 11 is not a Mote and adds no slot, so the drain holds 7 or 8 Motes.
- The drain runs 1801 steps (30 s at one step per tick). It reveals one slot every 1801 / slots steps in whole numbers: 225 steps for 8 slots (257 for 7). Each revealed slot shows that Mote's effect above the hero and uses 20 charge of that Mote (SPEC-0021 Mote charge). A Mote whose charge reaches 0 is removed from the inventory and unequipped from the active hero's equipment slots.
- **Who steps it.** Each sister's control loop advances the drain once after its state handler, including while state 9 plays the death animation. The death handler never changes the sister's base state to dead (at the end of the clip it only clears her body-push flag and her hero-candidate alive word), and nothing else does, so her loop keeps running after her death and keeps making this step on every pass (Medium-High; the only code that sets the dead base state is the shared death hook, and the take-hit puts her base state back to normal right after it). The active group's rate is therefore constant: all three loops step it on every pass for the whole fight, 3 steps per tick, whatever the living-sister counter says. 1801 steps take 601 ticks (about 10 seconds), with the 8-slot reveals 75 ticks apart. For the shipped arena region 1123, all three sisters' main action 26 selects clip 8: 50 frames with speed divisor S = 2. At baseline character and time scale 1, the HP-based rate gives a cursor advance of (2 − f) / 4 frames per tick, where f is current HP divided by maximum HP. The one-shot ends at frame 49, so the playback takes ceil(196 / (2 − f)) advances after it starts: 98 ticks at zero HP and 196 ticks when an overkill hit leaves full HP. A non-overkill lethal hit leaves zero HP; an overkill hit leaves the pre-hit HP, so its animation duration depends on that remaining fraction. The death-state duration therefore comes from the linked clip and current HP, not the alive counter.

**Leach (kind 15; High).** A Leach hit plays effect 4039 once at the hero's model point, then drains 50 MP from the hero (word 0x2DD458 = 50; MP does not go below 0, and the MP-empty rule runs). The missile's own damage is the separate word 0x2DAB7C (50). SPEC-0034 3.13 and SPEC-0029 (enemy projectile kinds 14 and 15) hold the shared rules.

### Taking damage (Confidence: High)

- A sister is hit by the hero's melee swing or by a hero shot. Both tests run after the dodge part of the hit and dodge test, on ticks when no dodge started.
  - **Melee:** the melee-hit latch must be clear, the hero must be in an attack state and the hero's attack-active word must be set (event bit 0x1 on a playing clip; SPEC-0040). The hero's combat reach test (SPEC-0036) is then made against one upright cylinder of radius 3.0 and height 6.0, standing on the sister's position and running up from it (no bone capsules). The damage d is the hero's outgoing damage for the hit (SPEC-0036 Damage) computed with the base power P fixed at 1.0 (the attack-power table is not used, so Tor's power step deals its swing damage with step factor 1.0), her defence 0 and her type 0, then truncated to a whole number; no absorb handling applies, so a result of 0 is a hit of 0 damage. Nothing else the generic swing resolver does happens for her: no hit-list entry, no magic or Mote charge payment, no Mote status request. The latch is set only on a hit and clears when the attack-active word is clear or the hero leaves the attack state. So one damage window lands at most one hit on each sister. A combo with several windows can hit again.
  - **Shots:** each of the hero's six shot slots that is armed is tested as a sphere (its point and radius) against an upright cylinder of radius 1.5 and length 6.0 whose base is 3.0 above the sister's position (the cylinder spans 3 to 9 above her; High). The test is the exact sphere-against-solid-cylinder overlap with flat ends. A cheap reject first drops a shot whose centre is farther from the cylinder's middle than 1.5 + 3.0 + shot radius. Beside the cylinder (its axial offset from the middle within 3.0) the shot hits when its centre is closer than 1.5 + shot radius to the axis (strict). Beyond an end, with the centre's distance from the axis at most 1.5, it hits when the gap to the end face is at most the shot radius; with the distance from the axis above 1.5, it hits when the distance to the end rim circle, sqrt((axis distance − 1.5)² + gap²), is at most the shot radius, so the rim is rounded by the shot radius. A shot exactly on the axis beyond an end counts when its centre is within its radius of the end face's centre. The routine builds this from two segment-against-sphere tests (the cylinder's side line facing the shot, and the near end face's diameter facing it); the end-face boundaries are inclusive (High; checked against a model of the routine on 200,000 random shots with no disagreement). A shot that hits is removed in every case, then the take-hit runs with the shot's damage d = trunc(S × C × T) (S the shot's damage scale, C the Nova bonus 1 + Mote stat G × 0.01 only for kind 7, T the shot's power-table scale; her defence is 0). The quick-shot magic and Mote-charge payment is made on the touch, whatever the damage (SPEC-0036, Roberta shot projectile); no status request is sent to her.
- A sister in a dodge state (missile or melee), the flinch state or the dead state takes no damage. The shield rule below still runs for her, so while the shield is raised a shot that touches a dodging or flinching sister still breaks it. With the shield down, such a shot is absorbed: it is removed and deals nothing.
- An attack state (4, 5 or 6) is interrupted by a damaging hit: the flinch replaces it. A shot not yet released is never launched, and that attack's cooldown is not redrawn, so the sister picks the same attack again on the first chooser tick after the flinch.
- **Shield.** At the start of the fight the sisters are shielded (one shared flag for all three, reset to the shielded setting when the main controller starts). While shielded:
  - a melee hit deals no damage, plays the shield flash (one-shot effect 4008) at the sister and puts the hero into attack sub-state 13, the recoil (it takes effect for Tor; SPEC-0036);
  - a shot hit deals no damage, raises level event 1 and removes the shield for all three sisters, for the rest of the fight.
- **After the shield is gone**, every hit deals damage d (the hero's damage for that hit):
  - d is added to the sister's damage-taken sum;
  - if d is more than the sister's health, she dies at once (dead state; her health stays as it was, which sets the death clip's speed through the HP fraction);
  - otherwise the boss name panel refreshes, the shared hit flash (one-shot effect 222) plays 1.5 units above her, a melee hit also plays the hero's hit cue, she enters the flinch (animation 23 or 24, chosen at random: whole-number draw 0 or 1), and her health drops by d. When d equals her health exactly, her health becomes 0, the shared death hook runs (statuses cleared; her rows and placements give no drop, so nothing spawns; it sets her base state to dead), and the take-hit then sets her base state back to the normal state and puts her in state 9 (this exact-kill path is the only one that runs the hook, and it makes no visible difference).
- Level events: 1 = shield broken (first shot hit), 2 = all sisters dead (see Shared bookkeeping).

### Group (dome) attack

**Start (shipped behavior: never; High).** Each frame the shared bookkeeping stores 0 into the group-attack start flag and then tests it. Every store to that flag in the boot program writes 0, so the chooser never enters the group attack, and the dome, its ring, its shots and its hit sub-state below are unreachable in the shipped game. A clean build reproduces this (no dome).

**Intended rule (held by the behavior but unreachable, because the flag it is gated on is always 0; recorded for reference; High for the test order and constants).** Start the group attack when all of these hold:

- every living sister's group-attack interval timer is 0 or less;
- at least two sisters are alive;
- the sum, over the living sisters, of the damage each took since her last group attack is at least 150 with three alive (word 0x2DAB40) or at least 80 with two alive (word 0x2DAB44).

Each sister's damage-taken sum is reset to 0 when her group attack ends.

The steps below describe the group attack as it would run if started:

1. **Begin**: every living sister moves to its slot on a ring of radius 25 around the ring centre. The slots are by sister index:
   - index 0 takes the ring centre + (25, 0, 0), a fixed offset along the world x axis;
   - index 1 takes the point 120 degrees around the ring when exactly three sisters are alive (the group word equals 3); otherwise it takes the ring centre + (−25, 0, 0), on the same world x axis;
   - index 2 takes the point 240 degrees around the ring.
   - Refinement (2026-11-09, High, from the code; unreachable in the shipped game): the ring-slot helper that places a sister on her orbit point during the dome is a separate routine from the entry placement above. For the sister whose station flag is 0 it takes one step of 5.0 per second (divided by 60) along the circle (tangent = unit(direction to the centre x up)) and projects back to radius 25. The other two sisters are placed relative to the sister whose station flag is 0 (found as Malice's record if its flag is 0, else Spite's, else Vanity's): with three alive, slot 1 is the centre + 25 x (cos 120 degrees x A + sin 120 degrees x B) and slot 2 the same at 240 degrees, where A is the unit flat vector from the centre to that sister and B = unit(A x up). With fewer than three alive, slot 1 returns the centre minus the UNIT vector, not scaled by 25 (an original slip; the entry placement above uses -25 on x). A rewrite that adds the dome may use -25.

   The ring centre is the hero by default, (the original can also take an anchor object; since the dome never starts in the shipped game, that choice is never exercised and the hero is used). Speed: the movement speed word (7.5 units/s, the 3-alive value) divided by 60 per tick, with no alive scaling (High). Confidence: High for the lane of the offsets (world x), the radius and the 120 and 240 degree points.
2. **Start**: once all are in place, a dome of radius 20 (0.8 × 25) forms. The first shot waits a random 0–3 s.
3. **Attack**, for up to 40 s (2,400 ticks) while the dome stands: each sister fires an energy ball (25 damage) every 3 s. The dome ends (2026-11-09, High, from the code) when ANY of these holds: the tick counter since the dome started is above 40 s of ticks; every living sister has been struck once during the dome (her dome strike count is above 0; this test is the "number of sisters with a strike count above 0 equals the living count" test); or the dome effect is no longer running. While a sister's dark-ball cooldown is above 0 she plays the idle clip 0x15; otherwise she plays clip 0x11 and throws the dark ball (the same setup as the normal dark ball), then the cooldown is 3 s. Her dome step 2 is a teleport onto her orbit point each tick.
4. **Hit**: a sister hit during the dome (the hit window is dome step 2; if her strike count is already non-zero the hit only moves her to step 3 and nothing else happens) moves to step 3: clip 0x17, drifting toward her orbit point at the ring travel speed (5.0 units/s, divided by 60 per tick) and returning to step 2 when the clip ends, then resumes attacking while the dome holds. The strike count rises by one on each hit that is accepted. The same dome-over test as above is applied in step 3. Dome steps 0, 1, 3 and 4 cannot be hurt. The dome is entered on the same tick by every sister (step 1 waits until the number of living sisters with dome step above 0 equals the living count).
5. **End**: when the dome ends, each sister sets its next group-attack interval by alive count (30–45 s, 20–40 s, or immediately with 1 alive), resets her damage-taken sum to 0 and sets her energy cooldown, then returns to idle (2026-11-09, High: the end also sets her dome step to -1, her strike count to 0 and the block's next-slot index to 0, and resets the dome effect).

## Edge cases and error handling

- Status ids passed to the hero: curse 1, poison 2, slow 3. Durations and effects belong to the player-combat spec.
- **No dome in the shipped game.** The group-attack start flag is always 0, so the dome never starts, whatever the timers and damage sums are. Under the intended rule a single surviving sister could not start it either (at least two alive are required), even though her group-attack interval is 0.
- **Shield until the first shot.** A hero who fights only in melee never breaks the shield: every melee hit is blocked and puts the hero into hit reaction 13. The shield falls only to a hero shot.
- **Overkill skips the flinch.** A hit whose damage is more than the sister's health kills her at once, without the flinch or the hit effect.
- **Pool full.** If the enemy projectile pool has no free slot at the release, the fired flag is still set and nothing is launched. The animation plays out, and at done the cooldown is redrawn as usual.
- **Chained attacks fizzle (quirk, reproduce; High for the rules, Medium-High overall).** Actions 17, 18 and 19 all map to clip 19. When an attack state ends, the state is 0 for the rest of that tick, and nothing requests action 1. If on the next tick the chooser picks another attack (a cooldown became ready during the previous attack, which is common below 0.66 HP), its request finds clip 19 already on its layer, finished and with the same mirror flag, so nothing restarts (SPEC-0005, Clip start). On the tick after, its progress reads 1.0, so the state ends at once with no projectile (the release test runs only before done), and that attack's cooldown is redrawn. The same applies to a dodge or flinch of the same variant that starts on the tick right after the previous one ended: no clip, no root travel, and the state ends the next tick.
- **Hold.** While level state 4 is set, the chooser forces state 0 every tick, even from a dodge, flinch or attack state, and the cooldowns are frozen. The hit and dodge test still runs.
- Malice's alternate routine changes the 3-alive speed word from 7.5 to 10, and the 3-alive energy range to 1.5 to 3 s, at its start and never restores them. Vanity's leaves the alive word at 3. So a main loop that runs later in the same power-on session moves at 10 with 3 alive, spawns with alive word 3 and uses 90 to 180-tick energy cooldowns with 3 alive (Controller routines).
- **Energy-ball speed depends on the loop.** The main-loop energy ball uses the speed for the alive count (0.5, 0.75 or 0.9). The alternate-loop energy ball always uses the word 0x2DAB94, which the alternate loop sets to 0.5 at its start, so its speed is 0.5 whatever the alive count.
- **Alternate-loop energy ball fires once per release.** The fired flag gates the launch. The alternate-loop decision clears it when it enters the energy-ball state, before the target roll. The energy-ball routine sets it at the launch and does not clear it.
- **Stored words are overwritten at runtime.** Malice's alternate routine (class 13) at its start writes 10 to the movement-speed word, 0.5 to projectileSpeed1, 1.5 to energyBallTimeMin1 and 3 to energyBallTimeMax1. Nothing restores them, so they keep these values for the rest of the session, including any later Sisters fight. The tables above give the disc values.
- **HP-based animation rate (High).** Each sister's class routine writes P = 2 − f after the NPC update's reset and status processing, but before clip re-time. The value therefore changes that tick's clip rate and replaces the earlier status multiplier in P. A lower HP fraction speeds the clip up; the later root-motion pass uses the root step produced at that rate.
- **Mote-eater drain speed (quirk, reproduce; Medium-High).** The drain advances once per tick for each sister loop that runs, not once per living sister and not once per tick globally. A dead sister's loop never stops (the death handler never sets the dead base state), so all three loops step it on every pass of the fight and the 1801 steps always take 601 ticks. Do not normalize it to a fixed 30 seconds, and do not slow it as sisters die. In the main arena the death clip has 50 frames and S = 2; at baseline scales its duration is ceil(196 / (2 − f)) ticks after playback begins, where f is the sister's current HP fraction (Who steps it).
- **Mote-eater drain of 7 (quirk, reproduce).** A pick that steps past the last Mote id adds no slot, so some drains hold 7 Motes (SPEC-0034 3.13).
- **Low-HP band falls through.** Below 0.33 HP a sister whose special cooldown has not expired uses the middle band (status missile, then energy ball, then move).
- **Orbit flips while blocked (quirk, reproduce; Medium-High).** The orbit direction flips on every tick the body contact flag or the navmesh blocked flag is set, not once per push. One push by a navmesh boundary edge keeps the blocked flag set for 120 ticks, so it gives up to 120 flips: the sister jitters in place sideways (an even count ends on the starting direction) until the flag clears or she is pushed again. Do not turn this into a single reversal.
