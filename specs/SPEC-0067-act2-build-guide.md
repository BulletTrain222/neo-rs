## 1. Summary and scope

This guide is the one place to read when building Act 2. It orders the work, gives the facts that must be reproduced, and points to the detailed specs for everything else. Positions are (x, y, z) in the pack named; flags are story flag numbers (SPEC-0049); "goal" numbers are the automated-player goals of SPEC-0058 ("Goal list for an automated player (Act 2)", goals 100 to 155). A short "default" after a fact is the choice to build when the fact is Medium or lower.

Scope. Act 2 runs from the end of the pack 1174 escape run (the end rectangle, trigger 1) to the Act 3 start at door 0 of pack 1327. From the act start on, Roberta is the only playable hero and the hero swap mode is 1 (Tor is parked, not switchable). The packs are 1174 (end only), 1300 (palace gardens), 1327 (library), 1335 (palace), 1344 (the wing north of the palace, a transition pack), 1324 (block and bell puzzle), 1299 (endless staircase) and 886 (the Act 3 arrival). Packs 1098 and 1174 are walked by Tor before the act start (goals 100 to 110) and are described only as far as the hand-over needs (sections 5.9 and 5.1).

The shape of the act in one paragraph. Roberta arrives in the gardens with no weapon and a small kit, watches the council gather, collects three wand parts (one from Painter Howell's chain, two from the ground), gets a wand and a practice lesson from Seradar in the library, sleeps in the palace (which turns the palace hostile), follows cries to a distraction puzzle in pack 1324 (after a mandatory visit to the wing 1344 that plays a lure movie), triggers three rescue movies, drops the west stairs, goes through the east door to the staircase pack, solves its pad puzzle, returns to the library and leaves by door 0 into Act 3.

What is hostile. Nothing is hostile in the gardens or library before the bed. After the bed (flag 92) the palace holds 13 hostile minions, then 14 after the west stairs fall; pack 1344 holds 9 minions once the hero has stood in it; pack 1299 holds 3 Evil Harris; the library holds 5 Biting Spellbooks on the way back. No boss is fought in Act 2. All enemies are common enemies of class 17 (SPEC-0038, SPEC-0064, SPEC-0065). No Act 2 goal requires a kill except the practice book of the lesson; Roberta cannot afford or survive a general fight, and some enemies cannot be killed by her at all (section 8).

Where the details live: hero setup SPEC-0053 (3.7), SPEC-0021, SPEC-0025; combat SPEC-0036; control SPEC-0033; death SPEC-0055; hazards SPEC-0057; pack scripts SPEC-0044 (1327, 1174), SPEC-0045 (1300, 1098), SPEC-0043 (1335), SPEC-0039 (1344, 886), SPEC-0041 (1324), SPEC-0037 (1299); movies SPEC-0026, SPEC-0052; streaming SPEC-0006 and SPEC-0030; music SPEC-0009 and SPEC-0031; effects SPEC-0034; cameras SPEC-0027; blocks SPEC-0048; pad puzzle SPEC-0028; shops SPEC-0050; petpets SPEC-0047; dialogue SPEC-0022; journal SPEC-0049; map SPEC-0051; ground and collision SPEC-0018; enemy AI SPEC-0038 and SPEC-0056; story goals SPEC-0058.

Confidence: High for the structure; each later section carries its own level.

## 3. Hero and inventory

3.1 Act start 2 (SPEC-0053 section 3.7). Runs inside the trigger call of step 4, in this order:

1. Hero swap mode 1; flag 220 on.
2. Tor's per-hero resources are released (his shot slots cleared, effect tree reset, sound bank slots unloaded) and the game waits until loading is idle.
3. Roberta is reloaded: her six resources are loaded, she is created in action state 1, alive, with her status timers cleared and speed scale 1.0. She is made the selected hero and placed at the current world's spawn with the spawn facing. The camera focus is bound to her; the status-apply record is cleared and the target list is emptied (count 0).
4. The party inventory and wallet are copied into a stash container: weapon, body, cloak and shield lists (2 slots each), general (20 slots), quest (60 slots), charm (14 slots) and the wallet.
5. The party inventory is cleared: every list is emptied, the wallet becomes 0, and the weapon-drawn flag becomes 0. The shield-raised flag is not cleared (it was set by the escape scene; harmless without a shield).
6. The kit is added through the normal add path, in this order: item 150 x3, item 149 x3, item 127 x1, item 135 with amount 1150. The add has no quick-item auto-select, so the quick marker stays -1. Item 135 is a coin: the wallet gains 1150, the coin cue plays and the price caption shows 1150. The caption survives the following world loads: its counter is cleared only by the pause or item screen, a slot load or the quit hold, and it counts down only on in-play HUD passes, so it shows for 180 in-play passes starting at the first drawn pass of pack 1300 (normal colour).
7. Roberta's health and magic are set to their maxima and her statuses cleared. Tor is not healed.
8. Hero attack objects are shut down, placed effects released, the petpet removed.

Not touched by the act start: both heroes' maxima, luck, Mote words, the quick marker, once-per-area item records, the story flags.

3.2 Roberta's kit and maxima. Items: Red Juppie (150) x3, Purple Juppie (149) x3, Diplomat Dress (127) in body slot 1 (outfit 0), 1150 Neopoints. No weapon, shield, Mote or cloak. The quick panel shows Red Juppie on Up and Purple Juppie on Down. Maximum health is 120 + 20a and maximum magic 150 + 25b, where a and b are the numbers of health and magic stat-ups added in Act 1 (every stat-up raises both heroes; SPEC-0036 "Roberta in Act 2", SPEC-0021). Regeneration targets int(0.1 x max) + 1 (13 and 16 at the start values), one point per 29 ticks (health) and 59 ticks (magic). Walk 13.51, run 19.29, slow walk 5.01 units per second, same as Tor. Luck is shared by both heroes and untouched by the act start (it decides the luck-gated pickups, section 9.5).

3.3 No weapon, no attack. The attack press does nothing while the weapon-family entry for Roberta (list index 1) is empty and the weapon-drawn flag is clear; she walks with the sheathed clips (ids 2 to 4). Line 66 in pack 1327 grants the wand and sets the drawn state (section 4).

3.4 The no-switch rule. Swap mode is 1 from the act start to the Act 3 start; it is written again to 1 by pack 1300 (on load and on each activation, while 248 is clear) and by pack 1327's slot thread. The L1+R1 chord is refused with no sound or flash; Select opens the pause page; no Act 2 script switches heroes. The Potion of Revival needs mode 2 and is unusable. The party portrait group is not drawn in mode 1 (its draw gate needs mode 2); the bars and icons are Roberta's (SPEC-0023): wand panel, robe panel and the magic-shield panel, the weapon icon only after she owns the wand, and the shield icon of the item strip stays hidden for her.

3.5 Death is game over. Roberta's death is refused a switch (mode 1), so timer E runs, flag E sets after more than 1.5 s, the game-over page shows, Cross is accepted from page pass 122 on, and Continue re-applies the last save or load snapshot (or runs the title flow if none is pending). Tor being alive makes no difference. Her death clip is the 64-tick clip (1.93 back) with voice cue 2. No Act 2 pack registers a hero-death callback, a kill call or a game-over call, except pack 1098 (only while story flag 30 is on and 31 off, Tor selected; section 5.9). Deadly ground exists only in pack 886 (one flat entry, no respawn index, so a fall is game over); no pack of Act 2 has hurting ground and no pack has a kill plane (section 11.1). Deep-water overlays exist in 1300 and 1335 but are shallow (1.2 above the floor, limit 2.3, SPEC-0055 section 9): wading is harmless, no kill volume is needed. Pack 886 has one big basin where the rule does fire. Damage sources in Act 2 are enemies, projectiles, the 1174 barrier zones and SPEC-0057 zones.

## 4. Roberta's combat

Sources: SPEC-0036 (Attack input (Roberta), Combo windows, Roberta shot projectile, Damage, Magic, Regeneration), SPEC-0040 (target list), SPEC-0033 (controls), SPEC-0021 (items). All High unless marked. Movement, animation and reactions are in section 7; enemy behavior and worked fights in section 8.

4.1 Acquiring the wand. Until Seradar's second talk in pack 1327 (items 10, 11, 62 held; flags 225 to 227 on; line 66), she has no weapon. The talk removes items 10 and 11 (item 62 stays), sets 228, 229 and the drawn state, and the dialogue grants item 111 (wand, attack stat A 5, magic stat B 100, reach 1.2, sound bank 469, visual step 0) and item 176 (Book of Shields, flat reduction 20 of a blocked hit; section 7.6). Item 176 also lies on the library floor at (-31.5, 54.9, -38.8) with carry limit 1, but that copy is on the unreachable upper level (section 5.3), so the dialogue is the only way to get it. Fewer than three parts gives hint lines 67 to 73.

4.2 Input. Attack is Circle or R2 (press edge). A press enters the attack state; a hold counter F counts ticks. Release at F below 80 is a quick release chain (windows: second press 20 to 40 ticks after the first release continues the chain; a press before tick 20 gives the rapid shot); F of 80 or more is a full release. The power-ready check runs from F 7; with no Mote it is always true. Without a Mote the full release is one class-2 shot (90 units per second, radius 1.0, life 180 ticks). A Fire Mote (item 160) in the Mote slot changes the full release to a Fire shot (75 units per second, radius 1.0, life 180, straight). Motes are equipped manually (second quick panel, Down); the script's 100 Fire Motes are not auto-equipped (resolved: no auto-select on any script add).

4.3 Launch. A shot is built when it has not fired, magic is at least 1, and the animation reaches its release frame. Release frames per sub-state: 2 at frame 3, 3 at frame 4, 4 at frame 7, 10 at frame 2, 11 at frame 3; the full release is set by its clip. Six shot slots; the first free slot is used. A hit on Roberta before the release frame cancels the shot (nothing built, nothing paid); shots already flying keep flying; a hit sends her to the hurt state unless she is in sub-state 1 or 9 with a Mote in the Mote slot. The quick chain in play: shots leave about 12, 37 and 70 ticks after the first release when the presses come at tick 20 of each step; a full chain cycle is about 109 ticks (a human presses later, so real cycles are longer).

4.4 Shot kinds. Quick class 1: 60 units per second, radius 1.0, life 180 ticks. Quick class 2 (sub-state 4): 90, radius 1.0, life 180. Rapid shot: 30 units per second, radius 1.0, life 120, homing blend 0.75 / 0.25. Full release, no Mote: 90, radius 1.0, life 180. Fire full release: 75, radius 1.0, life 180.

4.5 Costs. No Mote: 5 / 10 / 3 magic per quick hit (sub-states 2, 3, 11 / 4 / rapid) paid at each hit on a hittable target (undamageable targets still react and still charge; a shot that misses or is dodged costs nothing); full release 10 magic at launch. Fire Mote: quick hit 1 magic + 2 charge (about 49 hits per Mote); full release 20 magic + 25 charge at launch whether or not it hits. Maximum 150 (+ stat-ups); regeneration refills only toward int(0.1 x max) + 1 at one point per 59 ticks, so magic spent never comes back: after the lesson (which uses 30 to 45) about 100 remain for the whole act, and a plain kill costs about as much magic as the target has health. The Juppies of the kit (40 magic each) are the only refill besides the stalls.

4.6 Damage. Scale S = A x D x B x G x E (wand stat A; step factor D: sub-state 9 gives 1.5, sub-state 10 gives 0.33, sub-state 4 gives 2.0, sub-states 5 and 12 give 0.5, others 1.0; Mote scale B; status scale G; petpet boost E). Damage = trunc(max(0, S x C x T - defence)); C is the Mote bonus 1 + 0.01 x stat at hit time when the Mote's type matches the target type (Fire matches type 4 only), else 1; T is the power-table value for a full release (1.0 for quick hits). The first hit on a not-yet-alert enemy is doubled. Numbers for wand 111, defence 0, no Mote: quick sub-states 2, 3, 11: 5; sub-state 4: 10; rapid: 1; full release: 7 (S 7.5). The quick chain therefore deals 5, 5, 10. With the Fire Mote on a non-type-4 target: quick 5, sub-state 4 11, rapid 1, full release 10 (S 8.625 x T 1.25), plus the Fire burn 5 at 2, 4 and 6 seconds after the hit unless the target type is 1 (immune; Grunt and Magician are type 1); no Act 2 enemy has type 4, so the Fire bonus never applies in Act 2. A defence above the damage absorbs the whole hit (Dark Knight, section 8.7).

4.7 Aiming. The shot aims at the first record of the hero's target list: range 100.0, the character must lie within a cone of cosine 0.5 of her facing in 3D (no cone for a character already in her hit list within 6.0), the sight line must be clear, nearest first, the head record held for 90 ticks before a re-sort. With no valid target the shot aims at hero position + 20 x facing + (0, 2, 0) and flies level. The right-stick aim mode overrides. The boss forced-lock id and the melee lock list do not affect shots (no Act 2 code writes the forced id). A target's reference height is its placed height (a hovering book at 19.4 stays at 19.4).

4.8 Hits. Characters are hit through their bone hurt capsules (shot sphere radius 1.0 against capsule). World solids end the shot. Script triggers can fire with object 0 and shot word 1; in Act 2 the only trigger boxes a shot can start are pack 1335's triggers 5 (bed prompt), 8 and 9 (cries), and the volumes of 1344 trigger 0 and 1174 trigger 1; the handlers that test the object word reject a shot (so 1344 trigger 0 is never satisfied by a shot). No breakables exist in the Act 2 packs after the act start; the coin bushes of pack 1300 are cut by shots (section 9.5).

4.9 The practice lessons (pack 1327; SPEC-0044). Step flags 229 to 233. Roberta's health is refilled to full every tick during steps 231, 232 and 233 (not 229, 230); magic is not refilled. The book is a Shooting Spellbook (row 135): 35 health, type 5, defence 0, hovers 11.4 above the floor at (-28.3, 19.4, 27.5), state 0 until hit, fires 30-damage missiles every 100 to 250 ticks at a hero more than 10 units away. ## 6. Cross-cutting engine rules

1. Control threads and loaded second worlds (SPEC-0030 "Threads of a world that is not current", SPEC-0039, SPEC-0040, SPEC-0006).
   - Loading. A world that is only resident as a neighbour (the transition buffer) runs its Init at load, but its control threads, triggers, callbacks and Update spawn do not run; only a small fixed set of special threads is stepped. Consequence: pack 1344's minion set loads at the first step of its thread after it becomes the current world with flag 92 on (the hero walks in after the bed), never while the hero is in 1300, 1324 or 1335, and never before the bed. Reloads of 1344 (movies 20, 21, 25 in 1335; 23 in 1324) restart the thread and forget kills. The same rule governs Kekou's tour and the line-37 thread in 1335, which run only while 1335 is current.
   - Acting. The rule above only governs loading the set. Once the records exist, the loaded second world is ticked every pass after the current world (streaming, class update, motion, animation), in the same coordinate frame with no per-world offset, and hits on the hero apply at once. Bound 1344 minions therefore keep acting while the hero is back in 1335, but only where the zone query allows (below). Save-point prompts are not raised for the second world.
   - Zone query. The row is the hero's ground zone in the current pack's zone grid, the column is the minion's own ground zone, and the block used is the one keyed by the second world's id (3628 for 1344 as seen from 1335, 3625 for 1335 as seen from 1344). A record is ticked only when the matrix entry is 1. 8.2 Missile defence, the per-tick roll, and the hit-reaction lock. These replace the older per-shot "dodge N of 100" figures.

- Every agro tick, for every live hero shot whose straight path (position + per-tick step x look-ahead ticks of the row) meets one of the enemy's hurt spheres, the enemy rolls an integer in [1, 100]; below the row's dodge chance it dodges (outer sub-state 6, hold set to the look-ahead, dodge clip gate 37 / 61 / 57 by kind), then rolls the block chance. Nothing is latched. The check is skipped while the hold is above 0, in the hit reaction (outer 10) and in outer 2 with sequencer state 5 (recovery). The exit of a dodge sets the hold to a random value in the row's defence-timer range.
- So a shot that needs n ticks to arrive after it is first predicted to hit is dodged with probability 1 - (1 - q)^n where q = (ceil(chance) - 1) / 100. A quick shot flies 1.0 unit per tick, so n = ceil((D - 1) / v) rolls (v = 1.0 per tick for a quick shot, 1.5 for the finisher; Medium: the D - 1 offset follows the launch-to-target distance). Examples: Grunt q 0.09 at 12 units: 65 percent of first shots dodged; Magician q 0.49 at 25: 100 percent; Warrior q 0.99 at 15: certain; Spellbook q 0.29 at 10: 95 percent.
- A dodging enemy is in outer 5 or 6, which the target-eligibility test refuses, so shots pass through it while the clip plays.
- Rows: Grunt q 0.09, look-ahead 120, hold 120 to 300; Magician q 0.49, look-ahead 120, hold 30 to 120; Warrior q 0.99, look-ahead 60, hold 0 to 0; Biting and Shooting Spellbook q 0.29, look-ahead 180, hold 30 to 60; Evil Harris q 0.99, look-ahead 180, hold 60 to 120; Dark Knight q 0.74, look-ahead 60, hold 120 to 300. A hold range of 0 to 0 (all Warriors, and at dodge 75 the Drackonack and Rogue rows) means no open window at all.
- Hit-reaction lock. Every landed hit sets outer sub-state 10 again and restarts the reaction clip; in outer 10 neither missile nor melee defence runs and any swing in progress is abandoned. The reaction ends at the clip gate after the last hit: ground kind clip gate 49 (Warrior, Dark Knight), flyers gate 37 (Grunt, books), Magician gate 77, Evil Harris gate 116. Roberta's quick chain (presses at tick 20, shots 25 and 33 ticks apart inside a chain, about 51 ticks apart across chains) keeps a gate of 37 or more alive inside a chain but only the gates 77 and 116 bridge the gap between chains. So a Magician or Harris stays locked for the whole fight after the first landing; Grunts and books unlock between chains; a Warrior or Dark Knight unlocks after one chain. Medium-High (a human presses later than tick 20, so real locks are shorter).
- Consequences: Warriors cannot be shot dead (below). Shooting a Warrior is a stall, not a kill, and costs no magic because misses are free. Avoid Warriors: break line of sight and leave.

Source and confidence. The level-module code of every Act 2 pack (Init, Update, activate-world callback, triggers, threads), the placed-object tables, type-40 script groups and placed-effect tables on disc, and the state rules of SPEC-0028 ("Run-time object changes"); the original was not run. ### 16.1 Rules the tables rely on

1. States (SPEC-0028). Every placed object is hidden on disc (state 3: not drawn, no walls, no ground, no pushes). Only a module call shows it. "Start pose" means InitObjOn or ActivateObj with the end flag false: drawn, with walls, ground and pushes live on the call (state 10 on a clip of two or more keys, state 8 on a one-key clip). "Off" means DeactivateObj: not drawn, walls, ground and pushes gone on the call, and the colour reset to (128, 128, 128, 255) where the disc value is (255, 255, 255, 255). "Played" means PlayAnim: state 2 until the clip ends, then 8; walls and ground follow the pose from the next tick's object step.
2. Nothing is saved. Placed-object state, colours, offsets, collision word and effect switches live for one visit of a pack. Each pack's Init (and, where named, its Update or activate-world callback) rebuilds them from flags on every load. Init runs again on a replacement load (door, teleport, or a movie with a return world), on a movie hand-off that reloads that buffer, on a save load and on Continue. It does not run on the seam crossing between 1335 and 1344, and a hero death in Act 2 is game over (Continue is a load); only pack 1098 at stage 30 has a death callback that reloads its own pack.
3. Only the current world's placed objects advance, and only the current world runs its Update, triggers, thread tasks and breakable hooks. A resident neighbour keeps every object at its last pose, still draws it and still offers walls and ground. Calls without a world argument (ActivateObj, DeactivateObj, PlayAnim, SetObjToTime, the offset and colour setters) hit the current world; InitObjOn, LoopAnim, the effect calls, the slot calls and the mask calls name a world.
4. Frame order: object step, hero update, script tasks (Update, triggers, then threads), draw. A call that evaluates a pose or switches off shows in the same frame; a play call starts on the next tick.
5. Colour. A re-shown object keeps the colour it had. DeactivateObj leaves it at 128 on three channels where the disc value is 255; the draw treats 128 as neutral and 255 as about twice as strong (SPEC-0028, Looks). So an object that is off and later shown again without a colour call in the same visit looks darker than at load; an object shown by Init after a load looks brighter than the same object after a fade scene. The cases in Act 2 are in 16.5 (Medium for how large the difference looks).
6. Placed effects (SPEC-0034). All placed records are active from load and only follow the zone, cull, near/far and cone tests of their definition. StopEffect switches a record off for the visit; StartEffect runs one update and starts an emitter that keeps running. Record 8 of 1300 and records 3 to 8 of 1327 are started at Init only to run one update (they are active from load); a stopped record is started again only by the per-tick calls of 1098 (16.3.1).
7. Story layers (the level display tree, live every frame from flags) exist in Act 2 only in pack 886 (16.3.9); no other Act 2 pack has a type-4 node with layer flags. The world collision word is set only by packs 1174 and 886. The display word is set by none of them.
8. Seams and neighbours. 1344 sits in the transition buffer whenever the hero is in 1300, 1324 or 1335 (16.4).
